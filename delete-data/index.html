Delete your Swarm Survivor data

Swarm Survivor by Reezy Studios (Justin Radloff) · Last updated 14 November 2026

You do not need an account to play Swarm Survivor. If you signed in with Google Play Games, some data is stored online so the leaderboards and cloud save work. Here is how to have it deleted.

How to request deletion

  1. Email jradloff86+swarm@gmail.com with the subject Swarm Survivor data deletion.
  2. Tell me the leaderboard name you used in the game. If you have a Founder number, include it too.
  3. I will delete your data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your entries on the Weekly Runner and Weekly Tournament leaderboards (your name and scores).
  • Your Founder record (Founder number, hero and finishing time), if you have one.
  • Your player ID used for these online features.

What you delete yourself

  • Progress on your phone: open your phone’s Settings, go to Apps, Swarm Survivor, Storage, and tap Clear data. Uninstalling the game also deletes it.
  • Cloud save: open the Google Play Games app, go to your profile settings and delete Swarm Survivor’s saved game data.

What is kept

  • Records of purchases are kept by Google Play under Google’s own rules. I never see your payment details.
  • Anonymous gameplay statistics (Google Firebase Analytics) are not linked to your name, and Google deletes them automatically after its data retention period.

More details are in the privacy policy.
