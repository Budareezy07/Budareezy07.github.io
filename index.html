Swarm Survivor

Survive the swarm. Collect heroes. Save the four worlds.

Swarm Survivor is an action game for Android phones. Move your hero with one thumb while your weapons fight on their own, choose upgrades, mix elemental combos and beat huge bosses across four worlds.

Coming soon to Google Play.
