Privacy policy

Swarm Survivor · Last updated 14 November 2026

This policy explains what information Swarm Survivor (“the game”) uses and why. The game is made by Justin Radloff, an independent developer in South Africa (“I”, “me”).

The short version

  • You do not need an account to play, and I do not collect your email address or contacts.
  • Your game progress is saved on your own device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, it can also be backed up to your Google account.
  • If you sign in, the online leaderboards show the name you choose and your scores.
  • The game records anonymous gameplay statistics (Google Firebase Analytics) so I can improve it.
  • The game shows optional ads through Google AdMob, and purchases go through Google Play. Google may collect some device information for those services, as described below.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (heroes, levels, items, settings) is stored only on your phone. I cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Google Play Games and online features

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you sign in:

  • your progress can be saved to your Google account with Google Play Games Saved Games, so you can restore it on a new phone;
  • the game uses Google Firebase to give you an anonymous player ID linked to your Play Games account;
  • for the weekly leaderboards (Weekly Runner and Weekly Tournament), Firebase stores the name you enter, your best score and the time it was set. Your name and score are shown publicly to other players;
  • if you claim a Founder reward, Firebase stores your Founder number, the hero you used and the time you finished.

Choose a leaderboard name that does not identify you if you prefer to stay anonymous. I may remove names that are offensive.

Gameplay statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

The game sends anonymous events about how it is played, such as stages started and finished, puzzles and runner levels played, ads watched and purchases started, together with basic device information (model, operating system, language, country) and an app instance ID. This helps me find levels that are too hard and fix problems. It does not include your name or email address. Google processes this data as described at policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads, for example to revive or double your rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device’s advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your approximate location based on your IP address;
  • information about your device, such as model, operating system and language;
  • information about how you interact with the ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-game purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. I never see your card or payment details. Google tells the game only whether a purchase was successful, so the item can be given to you and restored if you reinstall.

Crash and diagnostic information

Google Play may share anonymous crash reports and performance data with me through the Google Play Console. This does not identify you personally. Google Firebase App Check is used to confirm that requests to the leaderboards come from the real game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing and selling

I do not sell your information. Apart from the Google services described above, no information is shared with anyone else.

Security and retention

Game data on your device stays there until you delete it. Leaderboard entries and Founder records are kept while the game is running, unless you ask me to delete them. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google’s own policies.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or South Africa’s POPIA), you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to correct it or to have it deleted. To have your leaderboard entries or Founder record deleted, follow the steps on the delete your data page, or email me at the address below and tell me your leaderboard name. Your Play Games cloud save can be deleted in your Google Play Games settings, and data on your device by clearing the game’s data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
